How to handle a dispute

  1. Confirm the protest was raised in time (during the match or within 15 minutes).
  2. Collect evidence — screenshots, the lobby log, and both captains' accounts.
  3. Message both captains from the dispute thread and give them a chance to respond.
  4. Record an official ruling with a written justification for the audit log.
  5. If unresolved, escalate to the Esports Coordinator (jlcabucos.cec@gmail.com).

Common grounds

  • Unregistered or ineligible player (IGN/ID mismatch, wrong division).
  • Unauthorized third-party software, bug or glitch exploitation.
  • Toxicity, harassment or discriminatory conduct.
  • Technical: disconnects, lobby sync, or reconnection beyond the 5-minute window.
  • Match-fixing, bribery or deliberate under-performance (immediate disqualification).
