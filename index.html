Three Divisions

STUDENT · MEN'S

College only · 24 teams

The official Intramurals competition. IT (8), HTM (8), CTE (4) and CRIM (4) each run their own bracket for a department champion.

STUDENT · WOMEN'S

College only · 16 teams

The official Intramurals competition, played alongside the Men's Division — four teams per department from IT, HTM, CTE and CRIM.

FACULTY · EXHIBITION

Friendly showcase · 8 teams

A non-competitive game for faculty and staff: IT, CTE-A, CTE-B, HM, TM, CRIM, SHS and JHS. Played Fridays, September 11 & 18.
