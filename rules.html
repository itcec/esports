CEC Esports Intramurals 2026 · Mobile Legends: Bang Bang

Rules & Guidelines

This document covers the Student Division (the official Intramurals competition), the Faculty Exhibition Game, and the SHS Showdown. It updates and supersedes any previous rules for this event. Organizers may amend these rules before a division starts, with prior notice to registered teams.

Contents

I

Overview of Divisions

A. Student Division — Men's (College Only)

The official Intramurals competition, open exclusively to college student teams representing their department.

  • Participating departments: IT (8 teams), HTM (8 teams), CTE (4 teams) and CRIM (4 teams) — 24 teams total.
  • Held every Saturday, 2:00 – 5:00 PM, starting September 5, 2026.
  • Each department runs its own single-elimination bracket to determine one department champion.
  • The four department champions advance to Grand Finals Day on October 2, 2026.

B. Student Division — Women's (College Only)

The official Intramurals competition, played alongside the Men's Division.

  • Participating departments: IT, HTM, CTE and CRIM — four teams per department (16 teams total).
  • Same schedule and format as the Men's Division; each department produces one champion.
  • The four department champions advance to Grand Finals Day on October 2, 2026.

C. Faculty Exhibition Game

A non-competitive, friendly showcase for faculty and staff, held alongside the Intramurals. It does not form part of the official Intramurals standings.

  • Participating teams: IT, CTE-A, CTE-B, HM, TM, CRIM, SHS and JHS — 8 teams total.
  • Held on Friday, September 11 and 18, 2026, 2:00 – 5:00 PM.

D. SHS Showdown — Senior High School

A separate one-day, in-person Mobile Legends: Bang Bang tournament open exclusively to Senior High School departments. It is a feeder event on the road to the Grand Finals and does not form part of the college Intramurals standings.

  • Participating departments: STEM, ABM, HUMSS, GAS, TVL-ICT and TVL-HE.
  • Held in person on September 11, 2026, 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM, at the Auditorium Hall, CEC, Inc. Jakosalem Campus.
  • All registered teams must be at the venue by 8:00 AM. Registration closes September 10, 2026.
  • Teams field five starting players and may register up to two substitutes, the same as the college divisions.
  • Teams coordinate their requirements through their respective Esports Head Manager.
II

General Guidelines

A. Eligibility

  • The Student Division is open exclusively to bona fide college students of the department they represent. It is a college-only competition.
  • The Faculty Exhibition Game is open exclusively to bona fide faculty and staff of the department they represent.
  • Each player may represent only one team for the entire duration of the Intramurals.
  • Player and team eligibility may be verified by the organizers before, during, or after the event.

B. Team Composition

  • Each team fields five starting players and may register up to two substitute players.
  • Substitutions may only be made between matches or during an official technical pause, with the approval of the match official.
  • A team that fails to field the minimum number of players at the scheduled match time may be given a default loss.

C. Scheduling & Punctuality

  • Student Division matches run every Saturday, 2:00 – 5:00 PM, from September 5 until Grand Finals Day on October 2, 2026. Each Saturday completes one or two departments' full brackets in a single session.
  • Faculty Exhibition matches run on Friday, September 11 and 18, 2026, 2:00 – 5:00 PM.
  • Teams must be online and ready to play at least 15 minutes before their scheduled match time.
  • Grace period before a default loss: 5 minutes for the Student Division, 10 minutes for the Faculty Exhibition Game.
  • Official schedules and bracket updates are posted through CEC Blue Dragon Esports' official channels. It is each team's responsibility to check for updates.

D. Code of Conduct

  • All players, coaches and team representatives are expected to display good sportsmanship at all times.
  • Cheating, unauthorized third-party software, exploiting bugs or glitches, smurfing (using another player's or a fake account), and account sharing are strictly prohibited.
  • Toxic behavior, hate speech, harassment and discriminatory remarks toward opponents, teammates, casters or organizers will not be tolerated.
  • Any form of match-fixing, bribery or deliberate under-performance ("throwing") is grounds for immediate disqualification.

E. Technical Requirements

  • All matches are played online. Teams provide their own devices and a stable internet connection.
  • Teams are encouraged to have a backup device or mobile data connection in case of technical issues.
  • Matches may be recorded and broadcast live; players must ensure their in-game names and IDs match their registered information.

F. Disputes & Protests

  • Disputes and protests must be raised to the assigned match official or the Esports Coordinator immediately during the match, or within 15 minutes after the match concludes.
  • Disputes raised after that period will no longer be entertained.
  • All decisions made by the Game Strategist/Analyst and the Esports Coordinator are final and binding.

G. Broadcast & Streaming Consent

  • By registering, all players consent to being recorded, photographed and live-streamed as part of the Intramurals broadcast.
  • Player in-game names, team names and department affiliations may be shown on stream, on social media and in other promotional materials.

H. Sanctions

  • Violations may result in penalties ranging from a warning, to forfeiture of the match, to full disqualification from the event, depending on the severity of the offense and consistent with the Blue Dragon Esports Manual.
  • The organizers reserve the right to impose additional sanctions as needed to maintain the integrity of the competition.
III

Bracket & Match Format

A. Student Division — Men's Bracket

Each department completes its entire internal single-elimination bracket (Best-of-1) in one dedicated session:

  • September 5, 2026 — IT Department bracket (8 teams)
  • September 12, 2026 — HTM Department bracket (8 teams)
  • September 19, 2026 — CTE (4 teams) and CRIM (4 teams) Department brackets

The four department champions advance to Grand Finals Day, October 2, 2026:

  • Semifinal 1: IT vs HTM (Best-of-3)
  • Semifinal 2: CTE vs CRIM (Best-of-3)
  • Semifinal losers play the 3rd/4th Placement Match (Best-of-3).
  • Semifinal winners play the Grand Final (Best-of-5).

B. Student Division — Women's Bracket

Each department completes its entire internal single-elimination bracket (Best-of-1) in one dedicated session:

  • September 5, 2026 — IT Department bracket (4 teams)
  • September 12, 2026 — HTM Department bracket (4 teams)
  • September 19, 2026 — CTE (4 teams) and CRIM (4 teams) Department brackets

The four department champions advance to Grand Finals Day, October 2, 2026:

  • Semifinal 1: HTM vs CRIM (Best-of-3)
  • Semifinal 2: IT vs CTE (Best-of-3)
  • Semifinal losers play the 3rd/4th Placement Match (Best-of-3).
  • Semifinal winners play the Grand Final (Best-of-5).

C. Faculty Exhibition Bracket

  • Eight faculty teams (IT, CTE-A, CTE-B, HM, TM, CRIM, SHS and JHS) compete in a single-elimination bracket for exhibition purposes.
  • Quarterfinals (Best-of-1): September 11, 2026 — 4 matches.
  • Semifinals (Best-of-3), the 3rd/4th Placement Match (Best-of-3), and the Final (Best-of-5): September 18, 2026.
  • First-round matchups are determined by an official bracket draw / seeding prior to September 11, 2026.

D. General Match Format

  • All rounds before the semifinals, across all divisions, are single-elimination (Best-of-1).
  • All semifinal matches are Best-of-3.
  • All Grand Final / Final matches are Best-of-5.
  • 3rd/4th Placement Matches follow the same format as the semifinal round (Best-of-3).
  • The SHS Showdown is the exception: its Final is Best-of-3, not Best-of-5 (see E below).

E. SHS Showdown Bracket

The entire SHS Showdown is completed in one day, on-site. Brackets are drawn and posted live at the venue on the morning of the event.

  • Elimination Round: Single Elimination (Best-of-1), contested by all SHS department teams.
  • Semifinals: Best-of-3.
  • Final (Championship Match): Best-of-3.
  • Round format and timing may be adjusted by the organizers based on the final number of registered teams.

IV

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Rules

A. Draft & Ban/Pick Phase

  • All matches are played using Draft Pick mode in a custom tournament lobby, unless otherwise specified.
  • The higher seed, coin-toss winner, or bracket-draw winner chooses side or first pick/ban priority, unless stated otherwise.

B. Game Settings

  • Matches are played on the current official game patch at the time of the event.
  • Standard tournament settings apply: no handicap, and no modified items or emblems outside of official game balance.
  • Using any bug, glitch or exploit that gives an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited and may result in disqualification.

C. Account & Player Verification

  • Players must use the exact in-game name (IGN) and User ID submitted during registration.
  • Account sharing or having another individual play on a registered player's behalf ("ghost playing") is strictly prohibited.

D. Pause & Disconnection Policy

  • The affected team may call for a pause immediately, through the match official, in case of a disconnection or technical issue.
  • Players have a maximum of 5 minutes to reconnect, or the match may continue without the affected player, or be forfeited, at the discretion of the match official.
V

Officiating & Final Decisions

  • All matches are officiated and monitored by the designated Game Strategist/Analyst and/or the Esports Coordinator.
  • In cases not covered by this document, the Esports Coordinator, in consultation with team officers, makes the final ruling.
  • These rules may be revised or amended by the organizers prior to the start of the relevant division, with prior notice to all registered teams.
