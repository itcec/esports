01

General Information

1.1 Eligibility

All participants must be actively enrolled students at a recognized collegiate institution in North America. Proof of enrollment must be provided via a valid .edu email address or official registrar documentation prior to roster lock.

1.2 Equipment & Accounts

Players must use their own personal Mobile Legends: Bang Bang accounts. Accounts must be in good standing with Moonton. Use of emulators, third-party controller mapping, or modified APKs is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disqualification.

02

Roster Requirements

groups
Active Roster

Teams must field exactly 5 starting players for every match.

person_add
Substitutes

Teams may register up to 2 substitute players. Subs must be designated before the tournament start.

2.1 Roster Lock

Rosters are locked 48 hours prior to the first broadcasted match. No additions or removals may be made after this deadline unless approved by administration under extreme extenuating circumstances.
