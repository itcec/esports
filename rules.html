CEC ESPORTS
Before you register

Rules

These are the public essentials. Officials may publish the complete competition handbook in the staff-managed records.

Eligibility

Student divisions are for eligible CEC students representing their department. Faculty & staff matches are a friendly exhibition.

Roster

Each team submits five starters and may include up to two substitutes. A captain is responsible for the accuracy of the roster.

Verification

Required identity documents are reviewed privately by staff. Only approved teams and opted-in public profile images appear on the public site.

Match format

Department rounds use the format published in the official bracket. Officials record scores, winners, status, and any published stream.

Fair play

Players must follow the official lobby instructions, respect opponents and officials, and report disputes through the tournament process.

Questions?

Ask the CEC Blue Dragon Esports tournament coordinator before submitting a registration.
