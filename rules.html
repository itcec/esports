CEC Blue Dragon Esports · Official Competition Handbook

Rules & Guidelines

This handbook covers the Student Division (the official Intramurals competition) and the Faculty Exhibition Game. All participants are required to read and comply with these tournament rules.

I · Overview of Divisions II · General Guidelines III · Bracket & Match Format IV · Mobile Legends Rules V · Officiating & Protests

I · Overview of Divisions

A. Student Division — Men's (College Only)

The official Intramurals competition, open exclusively to college student teams representing their academic department.

  • Participating departments: IT (8 teams), HTM (8 teams), CTE (4 teams) and CRIM (4 teams) — 24 teams total.
  • Held every Saturday, 2:00 – 5:00 PM, starting September 5, 2026.
  • Each department runs its own single-elimination bracket to determine one department champion.
  • The four department champions advance to Grand Finals Day on October 2, 2026.

B. Student Division — Women's (College Only)

The official Intramurals competition, played alongside the Men's Division.

  • Participating departments: IT, HTM, CTE and CRIM — four teams per department (16 teams total).
  • Same schedule and format as the Men's Division; each department produces one champion.
  • The four department champions advance to Grand Finals Day on October 2, 2026.

C. Faculty & Staff Exhibition

A friendly showcase for faculty and staff held on Friday, September 11 & 18, 2026 (2:00 – 5:00 PM).

II · General Guidelines

A. Eligibility

  • The Student Division is open exclusively to bona fide college students of the department they represent.
  • The Faculty Exhibition is open exclusively to bona fide faculty and staff of CEC.
  • Each player may represent only one team for the entire duration of the Intramurals.
  • Player identity and enrollment are verified privately by tournament officials prior to match approval.

B. Roster Composition

  • Each team fields five (5) starting players and may register up to two (2) substitute players.
  • Substitutions may only occur between games in a series or during an authorized technical pause.
  • A team failing to field 5 eligible players by the grace period is subject to default forfeit.

III · Bracket & Match Format

  • Preliminary Department Rounds: Single-elimination (Best-of-1).
  • Semifinals & 3rd/4th Placement: Best-of-3 series.
  • Grand Finals: Best-of-5 series.
  • All preliminary matches are played in assigned custom tournament lobbies.

IV · Mobile Legends Rules

A. Game Lobby & Draft

  • Matches are played using Tournament Draft Pick mode on the active official server patch.
  • Emblems and battle spells must conform to standard fair-play regulations.
  • Using unauthorized third-party software, scripts, or glitch exploits results in immediate disqualification.

B. Disconnections & Pauses

  • Teams may request a technical pause immediately through their assigned match official.
  • A 5-minute reconnection grace period is enforced before the match must resume.

V · Officiating & Protests

  • All matches are monitored and recorded by certified tournament officials.
  • Protests must be submitted to the match official during the match or within 15 minutes of completion.
  • Final binding rulings are rendered by Mr. Jade Louis S. Cabucos, Esports Coordinator.
