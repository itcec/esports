Session-day flow

  1. Draw and generate the department bracket.
  2. Open check-in — teams must be ready 15 minutes before their match.
  3. Start each match; monitor the custom lobby and score.
  4. Submit the official result and link stream feed in real time.
  5. Handle any dispute raised during or within 15 minutes of a match.
