BRACKET FORMAT

Department single-elimination

Each department plays its entire bracket in one session to crown a department champion. The four champions meet on Grand Finals Day, October 2.

MATCH FORMAT

BO1 · BO3 · BO5

Rounds before the semifinals are Best-of-1. Semifinals and the 3rd/4th placement match are Best-of-3. Finals and grand finals are Best-of-5.
